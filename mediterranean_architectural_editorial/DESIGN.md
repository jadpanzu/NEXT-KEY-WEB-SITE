## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-end Mediterranean coastal luxury integrated with refined European architectural editorial discipline. Tailored for an elite real estate agency rooted in El Menzah 9, Tunis, the aesthetic balances sun-warmed natural textures with calculated, modern precision.

The visual tone is discreetly affluent, trustworthy, and culturally resonant. It avoids garish real-estate tropes in favor of spacious editorial layouts reminiscent of high-end architectural monographs. Key signatures include warm mineral-tinted backdrops, stark nautical deep navy structural elements, balanced cerulean water accents, and earthy terracotta focal points that ground the experience in authentic North African architectural heritage.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the architecture and landscape of Tunis—limestone facades, deep Mediterranean waters, sky, and baked terracotta tiles.

- **Primary (`#258CCB` - Mediterranean Azure):** Represents vitality, sea air, and clarity. Used for primary interactive triggers, active tabs, link accents, and property highlight states.
- **Secondary (`#173D5A` - Deep Cartaginian Navy):** Represents permanence, structural solidity, and bespoke heritage. Used for dominant dark panels, navigation headers, rich hero framing, footers, and authoritative typography.
- **Tertiary (`#B34C30` - Terracotta Clay):** Represents craft, earth, and historical brickwork. Employed strategically for high-priority transaction badges (`À VENDRE`), status indicators, select CTA buttons, and warm architectural callouts.
- **Neutral Background (`#F8F5EE` - Sand Limestone):** The dominant canvas surface. Warm, non-reflective, and gentle on the eyes, replacing clinical white with material warmth.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`):** Reserved for elevated surface cards, modal dialogs, and sharp input backgrounds to lift content above the sand canvas.
- **Charcoal Text (`#222222`):** Primary body and title text on light surfaces for high-contrast legibility without absolute black hardness.
- **Soft Gray (`#777777`):** Secondary metadata, architectural specifications (surface area, room counts), and subtle outlines.

## Typography

The typographical pairing balances the prestige of European editorial publishing with modern functional clarity:

- **Playfair Display:** Carries emotional resonance, architectural gravitas, and luxury. Used exclusively for prominent titles, editorial property names, quotes, and section markers.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans:** A contemporary, geometric sans-serif with subtle humanist softness. It handles UI mechanics, precise square-meter notations, filter states, forms, and data chips without distracting from the photographic and editorial presentation.
- **Letter Spacing & Casing:** Small labels, transaction status badges, and technical tags (`label-sm`) employ uppercase styling with an expanded tracking of `+0.08em` for crisp readability against saturated backdrops.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout employs a structured 12-column fluid grid system bounded by a desktop maximum width of `1380px` to maintain comfortable readability and editorial cohesion.

- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `margin: 1.5rem` and `gutter: 1rem`. Search consoles and filter groups stack vertically into linear step flows.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with `margin: 3rem` and `gutter: 1.5rem`. Property grids collapse to 2 columns.
  - **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout with `margin: 5rem` and `gutter: 2.5rem`. Ample horizontal breathing room allows for asymmetric editorial showcases and multi-criteria horizontal filtering consoles.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows generous, deliberate increments. Whitespace is treated as a premium architectural material—sections are granted `space-2xl` breathing room to prevent density fatigue and elevate luxury perception.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids high-contrast digital dropshadows, relying instead on ambient, sun-diffused Mediterranean illumination:

- **Flat Foundation:** Base canvas (`#F8F5EE`) remains completely flat and matte.
- **Ambient Sun-Cast Shadows:** Raised surfaces (search bars, property cards) float gently above the sand ground using warm, low-opacity, wide-radius shadows tinted with deep navy rather than cold gray:
  - *Resting Card Elevation:* `0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(23, 61, 90, 0.06), 0px 2px 6px -1px rgba(23, 61, 90, 0.03)`.
  - *Hover Elevation:* `0px 16px 36px -6px rgba(23, 61, 90, 0.10), 0px 4px 12px -2px rgba(23, 61, 90, 0.04)`.
- **Search Module Elevation:** Floating interactive filter modules sit comfortably on hero images using a soft elevated shield: `0px 20px 40px -8px rgba(23, 61, 90, 0.12)`.
- **Architectural Ghost Outlines:** Secondary cards and inputs maintain structural crispness using a light mineral hairline stroke: `1px solid rgba(23, 61, 90, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The system uses `roundedness: 2` (0.5rem base radius), reflecting the balanced geometry of contemporary Mediterranean architecture—neither starkly brutalist nor overly bubbly:

- **Interactive Inputs & Buttons:** `0.5rem` (`rounded-md`) creates a tailored, tactile grip.
- **Property Cards & Search Bars:** `1rem` (`rounded-lg`) provides smooth organic edges while preserving geometric discipline.
- **Pills & Transaction Badges:** Pure pill curves (`9999px`) are reserved strictly for high-visibility micro-tags (`À VENDRE`, `À LOUER`, step badges) to create clear visual divergence from rectangular content boxes.

## Components

### Property Cards
- **Structure:** Encased in Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with `rounded-lg` (1rem) corners, framed by a delicate hairline border (`rgba(23, 61, 90, 0.08)`).
- **Image Treatment:** Aspect ratio 4:3 with subtle zoom transition on card hover. Top-left features transactional badges; top-right hosts an ambient circular bookmark trigger.
- **Information Hierarchy:**
  - Price in deep navy (`#173D5A`), styled in `title` weight.
  - Property name in `headline-sm` (`Playfair Display`).
  - Spec strip (m², bedrooms, location) styled in `body-sm` (`#777777`), punctuated with clean geometric icons.

### Transaction Badges
- **À VENDRE (For Sale):** Terracotta background (`#B34C30`), Pure White uppercase text (`label-sm`), fully rounded pill shape.
- **À LOUER (For Rent):** Primary Blue background (`#258CCB`), Pure White uppercase text (`label-sm`), fully rounded pill shape.
- **Exclusivité:** Deep Navy background (`#173D5A`) with a subtle 1px border of sand tone.

### Search Module & Filtering Bar
- **Surface:** Pure White floating container featuring `rounded-xl` (1.5rem) corners and ambient sun-cast elevation.
- **Controls:** Unified segmented control dividing "Acheter", "Louer", and "Estimer". Fields for Location (e.g., El Menzah 9, Ennasr, Gammarth), Type, and Budget separated by thin vertical dividers (`#E5E0D5`) rather than heavy boxes.
- **Search Trigger:** High-impact Terracotta (`#B34C30`) or Deep Navy (`#173D5A`) button with an icon and bold label.

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Action:** Solid Deep Navy (`#173D5A`) with white text, transitioning to Primary Blue (`#258CCB`) on hover.
- **Accent Action:** Solid Terracotta (`#B34C30`) with white text, used for high-intent conversions (e.g., "Demander une visite", "Estimer mon bien").
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Clear background with a 1.5px border in `#173D5A`, filled with `#173D5A` on hover.

### Step Badges & Numbering
- **Styling:** Circular tags (`36px × 36px`) filled with soft sand (`#F8F5EE`) or transparent navy outline, hosting serif numbers (`Playfair Display`) to visually guide clients through real-estate acquisition or valuation workflows.

### Valuation Estimate Forms
- **Container:** Sand background (`#F8F5EE`) card with deep white inset input fields. Inputs feature minimal `1px solid rgba(23,61,90,0.15)` borders that highlight to Primary Blue on focus.
- **Sliders:** Single and range track sliders for surface area and price brackets, tinted with `#258CCB`.

### Quote Blocks & Testimonials
- **Styling:** Left-bordered with a generous 3px stroke in `#B34C30`. Italicized `headline-md` typography in `Playfair Display` against the Sand Limestone canvas, signed with `label-lg` metadata.

### Header & Footer
- **Header:** Translucent white with backdrop blur over hero imagery; transitions to solid `#173D5A` on full scroll with gold/sand typography accents.
- **Footer:** Immersive Deep Navy (`#173D5A`) surface. Typography in warm off-white (`#F8F5EE`) and muted blue-gray. Features localized office coordinates for El Menzah 9, legal notices, and an architectural newsletter subscription panel.
